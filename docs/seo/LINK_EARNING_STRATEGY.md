## 2. Four Link-Earning Campaigns

### Campaign 1: Open-Source Developer Ecosystem
- **Asset:** Permissively licensed GitHub repositories (`@muxaro/source-adapters`, `muxaro-cli`, `ffmpeg-web-stream-inspector`).
- **Target Audience:** Developers, devops engineers, homelab hobbyists.
- **Backlink Mechanism:** Comprehensive documentation on GitHub referencing canonical deep dives on `https://muxaro.com/docs/formats` and `https://muxaro.com/docs/resolutions`.
- **Target Backlinks:** 150+ high-DR referring domains (GitHub, Awesome-Lists, npmjs.com, dev.to, Hashnode).

### Campaign 2: Original Data Studies & The MUXARO Codec Index
- **Asset:** Semi-annual report: *"The State of Web Streaming: Real-World Bitrates, Codec Distribution & VMAF Efficiency"*.
- **Methodology:** Automated sampling of 10,000 public streaming manifests, evaluating true bitrates, container overhead, and average VMAF scores across H.264, VP9, and AV1.
- **Outreach Targets:** TechCrunch, Ars Technica, The Verge, Streaming Media Magazine, Hacker News front page.
- **Expected Yield:** 40–80 tier-1 editorial backlinks per research release.

### Campaign 3: Expert Audio/Video Community Outreach
- **Venues:** Reddit (`r/ffmpeg`, `r/videoediting`, `r/audiophile`, `r/techsupport`), Stack Overflow, SuperUser.
- **Execution:** When users ask questions like *"Why is the audio out of sync after merging DASH video and audio?"* or *"Can I convert 128kbps AAC to 320kbps MP3 without loss?"*, our team provides comprehensive, mathematically rigorous DSP answers with FFmpeg command solutions, citing MUXARO documentation as the technical reference.
- **Value:** Builds authentic, unbannable forum authority, driving qualified creator referral traffic and natural citation links.

### Campaign 4: Academic & Journalism School Media Diagnostic Tool
- **Asset:** A clean, privacy-first media inspector that student journalists and digital forensics labs can use to verify whether video files have been altered, re-encoded, or truncated.
- **Outreach:** University journalism schools (Columbia, Northwestern, Berkeley) and digital forensics courses.
- **Expected Yield:** `.edu` authority citations and institutional syllabus placements.

## 4. Toxic Link Defense & Disavow Governance

- **Continuous Inbound Monitoring:** Monthly audit using Google Search Console and Ahrefs to detect automated scraper link farms or negative SEO attacks.
- **Disavow Policy:** Maintain an updated `disavow.txt` filed through Google Search Console only in verified instances of algorithmic warnings or malicious link farm spam.
