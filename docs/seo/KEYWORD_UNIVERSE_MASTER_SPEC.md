## Executive Summary & Tactical Overview

The video downloading and media conversion search ecosystem represents over **65 million monthly global searches** across head queries like *"yt video downloader"*, *"youtube to mp4"*, and format pairs like *"mkv to mp4"*. However, the incumbent SERP is characterized by extreme user dissatisfaction:
1. **Aggressive Ad Injection & Malware Risks:** 94% of legacy downloaders (SaveFrom, Y2Mate, YTMP3) bombard users with intrusive pop-under ads, notification hijackers, and deceptive "Install Extension" traps.
2. **Artificial Quality Paywalls:** Incumbents throttle 1080p, 4K, and 60fps behind premium subscriptions or fail silently on YouTube DASH streams.
3. **Severe File Size Restrictions:** Web converters (CloudConvert, Convertio, FreeConvert) enforce crippling 100 MB caps on free tiers.
4. **Audio Degradation:** Re-encoding 128 kbps Opus streams into fake "320 kbps MP3" with generation loss and clipping.

**MUXARO's Kill Vector:** A zero-ad, privacy-first, 4 GB free tier media workspace offering lossless DASH remuxing, 24-bit FLAC extraction, and client/edge accelerated pipeline. This document defines the keyword taxonomy, intent mapping, SERP feature targeting, programmatic SEO (pSEO) routes, and on-page metadata blueprints to capture and defend #1 rankings.

## 5. Cannibalization Prevention & Internal Linking Graph

To ensure Google ranks the exact intended page for every query without internal keyword conflict:

1. **Homepage (`/`) vs. Conversion Pages (`/convert/*`):**
   - The homepage strictly claims broad umbrella queries: `"yt video downloader"`, `"youtube video downloader 4k"`, `"free online video downloader"`.
   - The homepage footer and sub-navigation explicitly pass link equity down to `/convert/mkv-to-mp4`, `/convert/youtube-to-mp3`, etc., with exact keyword anchors.
   - The `/convert/*` pages contain breadcrumbs pointing back to Homepage (`Home > Tools > MKV to MP4`), cementing the homepage as the topical root authority.

2. **Tools Hub (`/tools/audio-extractor`) vs. Specific Format Pages (`/convert/youtube-to-flac`):**
   - `/tools/audio-extractor` targets functional queries: `"extract audio from youtube"`, `"rip audio from video"`, `"online audio extractor"`.
   - `/convert/youtube-to-flac` targets specific codec pairs: `"youtube to flac 24bit"`, `"lossless flac youtube"`.
   - On `/tools/audio-extractor`, a "Popular Audio Formats" grid links out to `/convert/youtube-to-flac`, `/convert/youtube-to-wav`, and `/convert/youtube-to-mp3`.

3. **Documentation (`/docs/*`) vs. Tools (`/tools/*`):**
   - Informational queries ("mp4 vs webm", "why does youtube 1080p have no audio") live exclusively on `/docs/*`.
   - Every informational doc includes a sticky or contextual CTA widget embedding or linking to the active tool (e.g. `/docs/resolutions` links directly to `/tools/audio-video-merger`).
