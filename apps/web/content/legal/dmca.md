# Copyright Policy & DMCA Takedown Procedure

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 26, 2026  

MUXARO Technologies (**"Muxaro"**) respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, authors, filmmakers, musicians, and copyright holders worldwide. We expect all users of our Service to respect these rights in full accordance with the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (**"DMCA"**, codified at 17 U.S.C. § 512), the European Union Directive on Copyright in the Digital Single Market (Directive (EU) 2019/790), and applicable international intellectual property treaties.

This document sets forth our formal procedures for receiving, investigating, and responding to statutory notifications of claimed copyright infringement, as well as the statutory rights of users to submit counter-notifications.

---

## 1. Designated Copyright Agent

Pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(2), MUXARO has registered a **Designated Copyright Agent** with the United States Copyright Office. All formal copyright infringement notices, DMCA notices, and counter-notifications must be sent to our Designated Agent:

- **Attn:** Designated DMCA Copyright Agent  
- **Entity:** MUXARO Technologies — Legal Affairs Division  
- **Email:** `dmca@muxaro.app` *(Recommended for fastest statutory processing)*  
- **Alternate Contact:** `copyright@muxaro.app`  
- **Physical Mail:** MUXARO Technologies, Attention: Copyright Compliance Officer, 1209 Orange Street, Wilmington, DE 19801, USA

## 2. Filing a Formal DMCA Notice of Claimed Infringement

To ensure your notification is actionable under statutory safe harbor requirements, your notice must be in writing and contain **all six (6) of the following elements** prescribed by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)(A):

1. **Signature of Authorized Party:** A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
2. **Identification of the Copyrighted Work:** Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g. title, registration number, or URL of the original work). If multiple copyrighted works on a single online site are covered by a single notification, provide a representative list.
3. **Identification of the Infringing Material:** Specific identification of the material claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, along with information reasonably sufficient to permit Muxaro to locate the material (e.g., exact URL on `muxaro.app` or specific job identifier).
4. **Contact Information:** Accurate contact information for the complaining party, including your full legal name, physical mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. **Good Faith Statement:** An explicit statement that:  
   *"I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law (such as fair use)."*
6. **Penalty of Perjury Statement:** An explicit statement that:  
   *"The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or am authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive copyright right that is allegedly infringed."*

> [!WARNING]
> Under Section 512(f) of the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(f)), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be held liable for monetary damages, including court costs and substantial attorneys' fees incurred by the alleged infringer or by Muxaro.

## 3. Operational Response & Takedown Timeline

Upon receipt of a substantially compliant DMCA notice meeting all statutory elements:

1. **Immediate Quarantine:** Muxaro will expeditiously remove, disable, or block access to the allegedly infringing material, or block the ingestion URL from our conversion pipeline.
2. **User Notification:** If technically feasible, Muxaro will promptly forward a copy of the infringement notification to the user who uploaded, requested, or initiated the processing job, informing them that access to the material has been disabled.
3. **Audit Logging:** The notice is archived in our encrypted compliance ledger for statutory recordkeeping and repeat infringer evaluation.

---

## 4. DMCA Counter-Notification Procedure

If a user believes in good faith that material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake, misidentification, or misapplication of fair use doctrine, the user may file a formal **Counter-Notification** pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(2)–(3).

To be legally effective, a Counter-Notification must be sent to our Designated Copyright Agent (`dmca@muxaro.app`) and contain:

1. **User Signature:** A physical or electronic signature of the user.
2. **Identification of Removed Material:** Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled, and the location (URL) at which the material appeared before removal.
3. **Statement Under Penalty of Perjury:** A statement under penalty of perjury that:  
   *"I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled."*
4. **Consent to Jurisdiction & Service of Process:** The user's full legal name, physical address, and telephone number, accompanied by a statement that:  
   *"I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located (or, if my address is outside the United States, for the Federal District Court for the District of Delaware), and that I will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification or an agent of such person."*

### The 10–14 Business Day Restoration Process
Upon receipt of a valid Counter-Notification:
1. Muxaro will promptly provide the original complaining party with a copy of the Counter-Notification, advising them that Muxaro will restore access to the disabled material in **10 to 14 business days**.
2. Muxaro will restore access to the material unless our Designated Agent receives formal notice from the complaining party within 10 to 14 business days confirming that a court action seeking a restraining order has been filed against the user.

## 5. Strict Repeat Infringer Policy (17 U.S.C. § 512(i))

In strict compliance with statutory safe harbor eligibility, MUXARO enforces a **Repeat Infringer Policy**:

1. **Three-Strike Standard:** A user or persistent identifier that receives three (3) distinct, verified, uncontested DMCA infringement notices will be classified as a repeat infringer.
2. **Access Termination:** Repeat infringers will have their accounts permanently terminated, their active API keys and tokens revoked, and their network access barred from the Service.
3. **Technical Enforcement:** Muxaro deploys automated IP range blacklisting, fingerprint matching, and domain blocking to enforce access restrictions against confirmed bad actors.

---

## 6. Prohibited DRM Circumvention Notice

MUXARO does **not** provide technology, services, or tools designed to decrypt, circumvent, or defeat technological protection measures or Digital Rights Management (DRM) technologies. In accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 1201, any request submitted to the Service attempting to circumvent encrypted streams (such as DRM-protected Widevine, PlayReady, or FairPlay media) will be rejected by our automated media worker pre-flight inspection engine.

## 7. Submission Checklist for Claimants

Before submitting a notice to `dmca@muxaro.app`, ensure:
- [ ] You have verified that the material is not protected by **Fair Use** (17 U.S.C. § 107; see *Lenz v. Universal Music Corp.*).
- [ ] You have included the exact URLs on `muxaro.app` where the infringing material is located.
- [ ] You have included both required statutory statements (Good Faith and Penalty of Perjury).
- [ ] You have included an electronic or physical signature.

*For all DMCA inquiries, contact: `dmca@muxaro.app`.*
